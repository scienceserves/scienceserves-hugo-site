# Privacy Policy

**Introduction**

At the Center for Science, Service, and Solidarity (CS³), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you visit our website (scienceserves.org) or interact with us.

**What Information We Collect**

We primarily use GoatCounter, a privacy-focused analytics service, to understand how visitors use our website. GoatCounter collects anonymous data such as page views and referrer URLs, which helps us improve the site's performance and content. We do *not* collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, email addresses, or IP addresses. GoatCounter anonymizes data by truncating IP addresses to a 16-bit value.

**How We Use the Information**

We use this anonymous data to:

*   Understand website traffic patterns.
*   Identify popular content and areas for improvement.
*   Evaluate the effectiveness of our website design and navigation.

**Data Sharing**

We do not share any data collected through GoatCounter with third parties, except for the purpose of providing and supporting the GoatCounter service.

**Data Retention**

GoatCounter retains data according to its default retention policies, which are designed to minimize storage and maximize privacy.

**Your Rights**

While the data we collect is anonymized, you have the right to request that your browsing activity be removed from GoatCounter’s records. Please contact us at info@scienceserves.org to exercise this right.

**GoatCounter’s Privacy Policy**

For more information about GoatCounter's privacy practices, please visit their Privacy Policy: [https://goatcounter.com/privacy](https://goatcounter.com/privacy)

**Contact Information**

If you have any questions or concerns about our privacy practices, please contact us at:

info@scienceserves.org

**Updates to this Privacy Policy**

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the updated policy on this page.
